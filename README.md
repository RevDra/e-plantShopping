# Paradise Nursery

Paradise Nursery is a premier online shop for high-quality houseplants. We offer a wide variety of plants to suit every home and skill level, from easy-care succulents to exotic tropicals.

## Features

- **Landing Page**: Welcome to our nursery! Learn about our history and mission.
- **Product Listing**: Browse our extensive catalog of plants, categorized for your convenience.
- **Shopping Cart**: Easily manage your selected plants, adjust quantities, and see your total cost.
- **Responsive Design**: Shop on any device.

## Technologies Used

- **React**: For building the user interface.
- **Redux Toolkit**: For state management of the shopping cart.
- **React Router DOM**: For navigation between pages.
- **Vite**: For a fast development environment and build process.
